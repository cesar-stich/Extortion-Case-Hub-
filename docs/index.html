THE PROBLEM

Bus companies pay to stay alive.

In Lima and Callao, gangs charge a daily "cupo" per bus. The threats arrive as voice notes from disposable numbers. Drivers delete them out of fear, and the police receive loose files they cannot verify or connect.

HOW IT WORKS

The company keeps its own evidence.

The company runs a UGREEN AI NAS at its depot. Our Skill is the conductor: it hands each job to the NAS's own expert agents, all read-only on the originals.

1

Drivers back up

Phones copy threats, screenshots and payment captures into a private folder per driver, over the depot Wi-Fi.

2

The Skill reads

Five NAS experts inventory files, read screenshots, transcribe Spanish voice notes, read chats and the route, and link camera events.

3

Reports come out

Every file gets a SHA-256 fingerprint. Reports show matches between drivers, escalation and what could not be read.

WHAT YOU GET

Four outputs, one source of truth.

 

FOR THE POLICE COMPLAINT

Driver report

Timeline, verbatim threats, indicators, cupos paid and a SHA-256 evidence index.

FOR THE COMPANY

Company report

Matches between drivers, an escalation level for each, total paid and incidents per route segment.

FOR OTHER COMPANIES

Indicator file

Anonymized accounts, file fingerprints and aliases to cross-check. No names, no files.

EVERY WEEK

Update mode

Only what is new since the previous report, so nothing is reprocessed.

ESCALATION

Four levels, always backed by evidence.

Each level is shown with the quote or image that justifies it. Facts only, no predictions.

1DemandA request for money, without explicit violence.
2Personal pressureMentions the plate, route, schedule, family or home.
3Violence threatThreatens death, injury, fire or shooting.
4Physical actShots, a damaged bus, a note left in person.
HARD LIMITS

A match is a fact, not a conclusion.

That judgment belongs to the police and the prosecutor.

  • Never names or identifies suspects
  • Never contacts anyone
  • Never advises paying, negotiating or confronting
  • Never files the complaint
  • Never edits, moves or deletes an original file
  • Never invents what it could not read
HONEST STATUS

The Skill package passes the official validator with 0 errors and 0 warnings. It has not yet run on a real NAS; testing it there is the next step. The demo and the images are illustrative, with synthetic data.
