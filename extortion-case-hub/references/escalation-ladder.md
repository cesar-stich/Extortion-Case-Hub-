# Escalation ladder

Back to [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md). Each member gets the highest level supported by at least one cited item. Always show the quote or image description that justifies the level.

| Level | Name | Evidence that qualifies |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Demand | A request for money or a cupo, with or without amount, without explicit violence |
| 2 | Personal pressure | The threat mentions the member's plate, route, schedule, family, home, school or sends a photo of the member, the bus or the home |
| 3 | Explicit violence threat | Words or images that threaten death, injury, fire, explosives or shooting, including photos or videos of weapons |
| 4 | Physical act | Evidence of something that happened: shots at a bus or depot, a note or object left in person, a burned or damaged vehicle, an injured person, or a camera event the member links to the incident |

Rules:

- A member statement (call memo) can support a level, but mark it as "member statement".
- A level never decreases inside one report; the timeline shows when each level was first reached.
- Speed of escalation is reported as facts only: "Level 1 on 3 Oct, level 4 on 21 Oct (18 days)". Do not predict future attacks.
- The escalation board helps the company decide whom to support first; the Skill does not decide it.
